Before the résumé

Your career is bigger than a job search.

Most career platforms begin when you're ready to apply. ROBUST begins much earlier: before the résumé, before the application, before the interview.

"What are you becoming capable of?"

ROBUST helps you move deliberately from exploration to specialization, formation, practice, evidence, and professional readiness, so instead of simply knowing the job you want, you can understand what that profession demands, and systematically build toward it.

Your professional formation blueprint

A profession is more than a collection of courses.

ROBUST breaks your target profession into the capabilities required to perform it.

Kn
What you need to understand

Knowledge

Principles, theories, concepts, frameworks, regulations, industry knowledge and domain knowledge.

Ts
What you need to know how to do

Technical Skills

Methods, procedures, analyses, calculations, workflows and technical competencies.

Tl
What you need to know how to use

Tools

Software, systems, platforms, equipment, technologies and professional tools.

Ps
How you need to operate

Professional Skills

Communication, collaboration, judgment, problem-solving, critical thinking, stakeholder management, leadership and professional conduct.

Ex
What you need to have practiced

Experience

Projects, simulations, cases, laboratories, challenges, internships, apprenticeships, field experiences and real-world assignments.

Ev
What can prove you're capable

Evidence

Projects, portfolios, assessments, credentials, certifications, work products, supervisor validations and demonstrated competencies.

Formation never ends

The world of work keeps changing.

New technologies appear. Industries transform. Roles evolve. Specializations emerge. Skills become obsolete. Entire professions intersect. Professional formation therefore cannot end at graduation.

ROBUST helps your professional blueprint evolve with the world around you: identify emerging capabilities, build new specializations, strengthen existing expertise, prepare for your next transition.
