ROBUST Business for Government & Economic Development

Don't just fund entrepreneurship. Build the infrastructure that produces it.

Every region wants more businesses, more entrepreneurs, more jobs, stronger local suppliers, more innovation, more resilient communities, more participation in priority industries. But entrepreneurs often encounter a fragmented system — training in one place, capital somewhere else, technical assistance somewhere else, procurement on another portal, mentors somewhere else, each institution running its own program.

ROBUST Business brings business formation and the entrepreneurship ecosystem together: build structured pipelines, develop founders, strengthen local businesses, grow priority industries, build supplier capacity, connect entrepreneurs to resources, coordinate ecosystem partners, and understand what happens after programs end.

The journey outlasts the grant

Follow the entrepreneur beyond the grant period.

ROBUST Business can preserve continuity across programs, providers, funding cycles and institutions — reducing duplicated services, since one entrepreneur may unknowingly receive the same introductory training from four organizations while still lacking the specialized assistance they actually need.

A legitimate economic pathway

Connect workforce development and business formation.

Some people need employment. Some want self-employment, contracting, or family-business succession. Some will move between these paths throughout their careers. Industry transitions can leave experienced people with valuable knowledge but uncertain pathways — ROBUST Business can help them ask:

"What do I know?"
"What industry problems have I seen?"
"What could become a service?"
"What businesses exist around my previous industry?"

Deep occupational experience can become the foundation for business formation.

For appropriate publicly funded programs, authorized reporting can help answer:
