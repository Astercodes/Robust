Discover Emerging Ventures

Discover what's being built before the rest of the market sees it.

Across universities and entrepreneurship ecosystems, students are identifying problems, testing ideas, developing technologies, building products, finding customers, and launching businesses. Most organizations don't discover them until much later.

ROBUST Business changes that — discover emerging ventures based on what they're building, the markets they're pursuing, the evidence they've generated, their stage of development, and what they need next.

Context, not a listing

Not another startup directory.

"Who experiences it?"
"What evidence supports the problem?"
"What market are they pursuing?"
"What have they validated?"
"What have they built?"

Not every claim is equal

See what has been validated.

ROBUST Business can help founders distinguish between:

01AssumedThe founders currently believe it.
02ResearchedExternal information supports it.
03ObservedThe team has directly observed relevant behavior or conditions.
04TestedThe assumption has been intentionally tested.
05ValidatedMeaningful evidence supports the assumption.
06Commercially DemonstratedActual market behavior — purchasing, contracts, repeat usage — supports it.

Partners can understand the maturity of the evidence instead of treating every claim in a pitch deck equally.
