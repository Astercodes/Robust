ROBUST Career for Recent Graduates & Early Career Professionals

Graduation wasn't the end of your formation. It was the beginning of your professional life.

You graduated. Maybe you're looking for your first professional role. Maybe you already have one. Maybe you're discovering that the profession is different from what you expected. Maybe you want to become better at what you do. Maybe you're preparing for promotion. Maybe you're choosing a specialization. Maybe you're realizing that the path you started isn't the path you want to stay on.

ROBUST Career continues with you after campus. Understand where you are. See where your profession can take you. Identify what you need next. Build deeper capability. Create evidence. Navigate opportunities. And keep forming throughout the early years of your professional life.

A new set of questions

Your degree is complete. Your professional formation isn't.

Graduation marks an important milestone. But entering a profession introduces an entirely new set of questions.

How do I get my first real opportunity? What am I still missing?
How do I become good at this profession? What should I learn during my first few years?
Which specialization should I pursue? Which certifications actually matter?
How do I move beyond entry level? What should my next role be? Am I ready for promotion?
What if I want to change direction? What capabilities from my current role can transfer somewhere else?

ROBUST Career gives you infrastructure for answering those questions throughout your professional journey.

A new arc begins

Your professional journey has changed.

04 — Advance

Don't wait for promotion to start becoming ready for it.

Professional progression should be visible before the next opportunity arrives. Your profession may offer multiple advancement tracks.

Individual Contributor Track

Junior → Intermediate → Senior → Staff → Principal → Distinguished / Fellow / Expert. Progression may require deeper technical capability, judgment, complexity, autonomy and influence.

Management Track

Professional → Team Lead → Manager → Senior Manager → Director → Vice President → Executive Leadership. Managing your own work is not the same as managing other people, functions, resources and strategy.

Specialist Track

Generalist → Specialist → Senior Specialist → Principal Specialist → Subject-Matter Expert → Enterprise / Industry Expert. Some professionals advance through expertise rather than management.

Not one generic ladder

Other professions have different ladders.

Academic careers may progress differently from engineering. Clinical careers differently from consulting. Skilled trades differently from finance. Public service differently from technology. Research differently from corporate management.

ROBUST Career maps progression according to the profession rather than forcing everyone into one generic career ladder.

05 — Transition

Changing direction shouldn't mean throwing away everything you've built.

People change careers. Industries change. Technology changes. Organizations restructure. Some professions shrink. New professions emerge. Personal priorities change. Sometimes the right next move isn't upward. It's sideways. Or somewhere completely different.

ROBUST Career helps you understand the transition.
