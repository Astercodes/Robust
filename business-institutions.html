The architecture

FIRSTS Business builds the foundation. IBFS builds the specialization.

ROBUST Business manages the formation.

Fb

FIRSTS Business

Give students the common business foundation every aspiring entrepreneur needs — opportunity discovery, customers, markets, business models, finance, marketing, sales, operations, leadership, strategy, execution and growth.

Ib

IBFS

Students enter the Individualized Business Formation Speciality appropriate to the business world they want to pursue — increasingly specific to their industry, sector, specialty, business type, model, customer, geography, opportunity and venture.

Rb

ROBUST Business

Your institution gains the digital infrastructure for delivering, coordinating, tracking, measuring and continuously improving the entire formation journey.

Not pieced together alone

Give every student a Business Formation Map.

ROBUST Business can generate a structured formation pathway showing:

Where they are

Their existing knowledge, experiences, capabilities and business progress.

Where they're going

Their entrepreneurial interests, chosen specialty, opportunity or existing venture.

What their destination requires

The knowledge, skills, capabilities, resources, relationships, experiences and business infrastructure associated with that pathway.

What gaps exist

What they haven't yet learned, practiced, demonstrated, accessed or built.

What comes next

Recommended learning, projects, experiences, mentors, resources and actions.

The result is an individualized Business Formation Plan that can evolve with the student.

The problem is fragmentation

From entrepreneurship programs to an entrepreneurship operating system.

Your institution may already have excellent resources. The problem may not be the absence of programs — it may be fragmentation.

Another operates an incubator.

Advancement knows successful alumni.

Students run entrepreneurship clubs.
Faculty have industry connections.

Trust is infrastructure too

Protect student agency.

Students remain at the center of their formation. ROBUST Business should be designed with privacy and appropriate access controls built into the institutional experience. Students can understand:

What information existsWho can access itWhat they choose to shareWhat belongs to their institutional recordWhat belongs to their personal business portfolio

Sensitive venture information should not automatically become visible simply because a student participates in an institutional program.
