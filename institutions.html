ROBUST for Higher Education

Don't wait until graduation to prepare students for professional life.

Your students are already becoming professionals. The question is whether that formation is intentional, structured, visible, measurable, and connected to where the world of work is going.

ROBUST gives colleges and universities the infrastructure to build professional formation alongside academic education — from a student's first semester through graduation and beyond.

A changing world of work

Create a living bridge between curriculum and a changing world of work.

Professions evolve. New technologies emerge. Tools change. Industries transform. New specialties appear. ROBUST's professional taxonomy and capability architecture can help institutions monitor how professional requirements are changing — and examine where emerging capabilities intersect with existing programs and student development opportunities.

The goal isn't to turn universities into job-training centers. It is to give academic education a clearer connection to the professional worlds students may eventually enter.
