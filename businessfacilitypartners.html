ROBUST Business for Facility, Technology & Knowledge Partners

Give founders access to what they need to actually build.

An idea can begin anywhere. Building it cannot. A food founder may need a commercial kitchen. A hardware founder may need a fabrication lab. A biotechnology venture may need laboratory access. A fashion founder may need production equipment. A software company may need cloud infrastructure. An AI venture may need datasets and computing resources. A manufacturer may need prototyping equipment. A founder entering a regulated industry may need standards, research, technical documentation, and specialist knowledge.

Business formation requires infrastructure. ROBUST Business connects student founders across participating campuses with the facilities, technologies, tools, information, and knowledge resources required to move from idea to operating business.

Beyond curriculum

The next thing a founder needs isn't always another class.

Sometimes they need a laboratory, a commercial kitchen, a machine, a studio, a testing facility, a software license, cloud computing, a dataset, a research database, a technical standard, a market report, specialized equipment, a simulation environment, a production facility, or access to someone who knows how to use it.

ROBUST Business helps connect those resources directly to the business formation journey.

Where resources meet the journey

Become part of the infrastructure behind student entrepreneurship.

ROBUST Business runs IBFS, the Individualized Business Formation Speciality, across participating campuses as an end-to-end student incubation and acceleration program. Students move through:

Explore→Discover→Validate→Incubate→Build→Test→Launch→Operate→Accelerate

At different points, their progress depends on resources that universities and incubators cannot always provide alone. That's where Facility, Technology & Knowledge Partners enter the ecosystem.

One connected formation network

Three kinds of infrastructure.

Facility Partners

Give founders access to the physical environments required to build, test, produce, demonstrate, or operate.

Technology Partners

Give founders access to the digital tools, platforms, software, infrastructure, equipment, and technical systems required to develop their businesses.

Knowledge Partners

Give founders access to specialized information, research, standards, publications, datasets, training, methodologies, and industry knowledge.
