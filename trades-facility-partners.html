ROBUST Trades for Facility, Technology & Knowledge Partners

Skills cannot be built in theory alone. Give tradespeople the places, tools, and knowledge to become capable.

A student can watch a welding lesson. But eventually, they need to weld. They can study electrical systems. But eventually, they need to wire, test, diagnose, and troubleshoot. They can learn HVAC theory. But eventually, they need to work with actual systems. They can study machining. But eventually, they need access to machines.

Real trade capability requires places to practice, tools to use, equipment to operate, technology to understand, standards to follow, knowledge to apply, problems to solve, and environments that resemble the real world. ROBUST Trades connects facility providers, manufacturers, equipment companies, software and technology providers, training-content organizations, publishers, simulation providers, assessment organizations, laboratories, makerspaces, technical centers, and other infrastructure partners with institutions and learners developing the next generation of skilled talent.

Training requires infrastructure

Trade formation happens across multiple environments.

A classroom can develop knowledge. A simulation can develop understanding. A lab can develop familiarity. A workshop can develop technique. A training center can develop practical capability. A jobsite can develop professional competence. ROBUST Trades helps connect these environments into one formation journey.

Learn→Simulate→Practice→Perform→Demonstrate→Verify→Experience→Work
