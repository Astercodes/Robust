ROBUST Business for Employers & Partners

Help build the businesses, founders, and capabilities your industry will need next.

Universities are full of students exploring industries, developing ideas, solving problems, and building businesses. But too often, industry enters the picture only at the end — at a career fair, at a pitch competition, or when a startup is already looking for funding.

ROBUST Business brings industry into the formation process much earlier: share expertise, bring real problems, develop emerging founders, open your industry to students, discover new ideas, create innovation pathways, and find future suppliers, partners, employees, and ventures.

Proximity matters

Industry shouldn't sit outside entrepreneurship education.

A student can learn business fundamentals in a classroom. But understanding an industry requires proximity to the people actually operating inside it.

"How does procurement really work?"
"What makes a supplier credible?"
"What regulations shape the market?"
"Where are margins under pressure?"
"What technologies are changing operations?"
"What problems remain poorly solved?"
"What mistakes do new entrants repeatedly make?"
"What separates strong operators from everyone else?"

Those insights live inside industry. ROBUST Business creates structured ways for companies and professionals to bring them into student formation.

Beyond "who can we hire?"

You're not just recruiting talent. You're helping form it.

"Who can we hire?"

  • What new businesses does our value chain need?
  • What supplier gaps exist?
  • Where could student innovation create value?
  • Which emerging ventures should we know about?

Industry partnership becomes a long-term capability and ecosystem strategy.

See it, then simulate it

Bring students inside your operations.

Some business concepts only become understandable when students see them:

Site visitsFacility toursJob shadowingOperational demonstrationsInnovation-center visitsManufacturing toursLaboratory experiencesField experiencesExecutive conversationsSupplier toursVirtual operations walkthroughs

Not every experience requires sensitive information. Create simulations from realistic decisions:

A supplier fails days before production.
A major customer threatens to leave.
Demand suddenly doubles.
A competitor cuts prices.
A product fails quality inspection.
A regulation changes.
Cash flow becomes constrained.
A cybersecurity incident disrupts operations.

Students make decisions and experience simulated consequences — developing judgment before the stakes become real.
