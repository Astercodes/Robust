Recruiting starts too late

The creative talent pipeline shouldn't begin with a job posting.

Traditional recruiting often begins too late. A role opens. A job description is posted. Hundreds of résumés arrive. Portfolios are reviewed. Interviews begin. And employers try to determine:

Who can actually create?
Who understands the craft?
Who can take feedback?
Who knows how to collaborate?
Who can work through ambiguity?
Who can move from concept to finished work?

ROBUST Creative moves that relationship upstream.

Instead of

Graduate → Apply → Portfolio Review → Interview → Hire

Build

Discover → Engage → Mentor → Challenge → Observe → Review → Experience → Recruit

Meet the creative before you need the employee.

As deep as you want to go

Meet talent before graduation.

ROBUST gives industry partners multiple ways to participate depending on how deeply they want to engage.

Expose

Help students understand your industry: career talks, studio tours, production visits, behind-the-scenes sessions, industry panels, technology demonstrations, role showcases.

Teach

Bring professional practice into formation: masterclasses, workshops, technical demonstrations, production walkthroughs, tool training, case studies, industry sessions.

Mentor

Support developing creatives: individual mentorship, group mentorship, project guidance, creative coaching, industry navigation, professional development.

Critique

Help students see their work through professional eyes: portfolio reviews, project critique, demo reviews, showreel reviews, audition feedback, pitch reviews, script feedback, production reviews.

Challenge

Give students meaningful problems to solve: creative challenges, industry simulations, production scenarios, design challenges, hackathons, game jams, competitions, creative briefs.

Host

Bring learners into real environments: job shadowing, internships, production placements, residencies, studio placements, co-ops, work-based learning.

Recruit

Build relationships with talent you've already seen develop: interns, freelancers, contractors, apprentices, production crew, entry-level hires, graduate hires, specialized creative talent.
