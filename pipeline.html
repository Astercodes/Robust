Step 12

Assess what matters.

Define assessment points throughout the pipeline. Assess:

Technical capabilityKnowledgeProblem solvingProfessional judgmentCommunicationCollaborationIndustry understandingQuality of executionToolsAnalysisProfessional behavior

Assessment doesn't have to happen only at the end. Capability can become progressively visible as the person develops.

Not all evidence is equal

Different evidence carries different weight.

ROBUST can distinguish between:

01Declared"I have this skill."
02Learned"I completed relevant formation."
03Practiced"I have exercised this capability."
04Assessed"My performance was evaluated."
05Demonstrated"Here is evidence of my application."
06Supervisor-Validated"A qualified supervisor observed it."
07Employer-Validated"An employer validated it in an appropriate professional context."

This creates richer talent signals than a skills list alone.

Worked example

Build a Reservoir Engineering Talent Pipeline.

01Workforce NeedYour organization expects increased demand for reservoir engineering capability over the next five years.
02Target ProfessionReservoir Engineering.
03Capability ProfileReservoir geology, petrophysics, fluid properties, material balance, well testing, reservoir simulation, production forecasting, reserves estimation, field development, economics, technical communication.
04ToolsRelevant reservoir modeling and simulation tools, data analysis tools, industry software, programming and visualization tools where appropriate.
05Talent SourcesRelevant engineering and geoscience programs, graduate programs, adjacent technical disciplines, existing employees seeking specialization.
06Formation TrackIndustry foundations, reservoir fundamentals, technical modules, tool practice, case studies, simulation exercises, field-development project.
07Employer ExperiencesMentorship, technical seminars, reservoir challenge, subsurface simulation, internship, capstone collaboration.
08EvidenceReservoir model, forecast, technical report, field-development recommendation, presentation.
09ValidationTechnical assessment, project review, mentor feedback, internship supervisor validation.
10Talent CommunityParticipants continue developing and engaging with your organization.
11OpportunityEligible participants may pursue internships, graduate programs and relevant professional roles as opportunities become available.
