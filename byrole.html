The Student

A journey, not a transaction

One student. Many contributors.

Imagine Maya, a first-year engineering student.

Y1Year OneHer advisor helps her explore professional pathways. She discovers renewable-energy engineering through ROBUST.
Y2Year TwoHer faculty courses begin contributing capabilities to her formation record. She joins an engineering organization and develops leadership experience.
Y3Year ThreeCareer Services identifies an experience gap. Experiential Learning connects her to an employer challenge. An alumni mentor helps her understand the industry.
Y4Year FourShe completes an internship. Her supervisor validates relevant capabilities. Her capstone becomes professional evidence. She graduates with a visible record of how she has formed professionally.

Every participating institutional team contributed without needing to operate as an isolated system.

What the student experiences

From fragmented services to connected formation.

Without a shared formation layer:

Faculty knows the coursework.
Career Services knows the career conversations.
Advisors know the academic decisions.
Experiential Learning knows the internships.
Student Affairs knows the co-curricular experiences.
Employer Relations knows the employers.
Alumni Relations knows the graduates.
Institutional Research knows the institutional data.

But the student experiences all of it. ROBUST connects these pieces around the student's professional formation journey.
