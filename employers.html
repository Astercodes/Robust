ROBUST for Employers

Don't wait until you need talent to start building it.

Traditional recruiting begins with a vacancy: post the role, collect applications, screen résumés, interview candidates, try to determine who can actually do the work.

ROBUST gives employers another way — participate in professional formation before recruitment begins. Help emerging professionals understand your industry, develop relevant capabilities, practice real work, solve meaningful problems, and build evidence of what they can do.

Then, when opportunity comes, you're not meeting talent for the first time. You've already seen them develop.

Beyond the résumé

Recruiting tells you who applied. Formation helps you understand who is becoming capable.

A résumé can tell you where someone studied, where they worked, what they say they know, what credentials they hold. But employers need deeper answers.

"Can they actually perform?"
"How do they think?"
"Can they solve problems?"
"Can they communicate?"
"Can they apply what they know?"
"Have they practiced anything resembling the work?"
"What evidence supports their claims?"
"Where are their capability gaps?"

ROBUST is designed to make professional development more visible before hiring decisions have to be made.
