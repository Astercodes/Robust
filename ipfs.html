IPFS — Individual Professional Formation Program

We bring professional formation to campus. From first career direction to professional capability.

IPFS is a structured professional formation program run by ROBUST across colleges and universities to help students deliberately prepare for the professions they want to enter — so they don't have to figure out professional development alone.

Discover where they are going. Choose a pathway. Understand what their profession requires. Build the required capabilities. Practice professional work. Gain experience. Build evidence. Prepare for the transition from student to professional. All through one structured campus-wide formation program.

Not concentrated near graduation

What if professional formation happened throughout college?

Students spend years earning their degrees. But professional preparation is often concentrated near graduation — a career appointment, a résumé workshop, a career fair, an internship, a few employer events. Then applications begin.

We believe professional formation should begin earlier, happen continuously, connect to the profession the student wants to enter, and integrate across the campus. That's why we created IPFS.

Academic Education + Professional Formation = students who understand what they know, what they can do, where they are going, and what they still need to develop.

The university continues doing what it does best: educating students. IPFS adds another intentional layer: forming professionals.

A common formation architecture

We don't replace Career Services. We give professional formation an institutional infrastructure.

Career centers already do important work. Faculty already develop capabilities. Advisors already guide students. Employers already engage campuses. Alumni already mentor. Students already gain experiences. The problem is that these activities can remain fragmented.

Career exploration→Professional pathway→Capability requirements→Development→Practice→Experience→Evidence→Assessment→Validation→Professional transition

IPFS provides a common formation architecture connecting them.

A simple question

Students enter IPFS asking: "What could I become?"

Through ROBUST, students explore the world of professions beyond the handful of careers they already know. They can navigate:

Career Worlds→Domains→Career Families→Pathways→Sub-pathways→Professions→Specializations→Roles→Positions
"I want to work in business."→Strategy → Corporate Strategy → Growth Strategy → Strategy Analyst
"I want to work in energy."→Oil & Gas → Upstream → Petroleum Engineering → Reservoir Engineering → Reservoir Engineer

Career exploration becomes progressively more specific. Then we help them answer the harder question: what does it take to become one?

One student's journey

Imagine IPFS across an entire campus.

A first-year student begins exploring professions. A sophomore chooses a professional pathway. A faculty member connects a project to relevant capabilities. An advisor helps the student make an intentional academic choice. An alumnus explains how the profession actually works. An employer introduces a challenge. The student completes a simulation. Career Services notices an experience gap. Experiential Learning connects the student to an internship. A supervisor validates demonstrated capability. The student completes a capstone and adds it to their evidence portfolio.

By graduation, that student can explain what they want to become, what the profession requires, what they know, what they can do, what they've practiced and experienced, what they've produced, what has been assessed and validated — and what they still need to develop. That's what we mean by professional formation.
